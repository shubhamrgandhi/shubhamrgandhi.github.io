I am a first-year Ph.D. student at the Language Technologies Institute of Carnegie Mellon University, where I am advised by Carolyn Rosé and work on LLM agents for software engineering. I completed my M.S. in Intelligent Information Systems at CMU in December 2025, and my B.E. in Computer Science at BITS Pilani, Goa.

I have spent two summers at IBM Research working with Jason Tsay, Kiran Kate, and Yara Rizk on process supervision for SWE agents. Before CMU, I was a Research Fellow at TCS Research with Manasi Patwardhan and Gautam Shroff, and a Research Intern at Microsoft Research India with Nagarajan Natarajan and Sriram Rajamani.

My research asks how to make long-horizon coding agents reliable and secure:

  • Process supervision for long-horizon agents. I build process reward models and rubric-based credit assignment that course-correct agent trajectories at inference time and provide fine-grained training signal, and I study which training environments help coding agents generalize.
    SWE-PRM (SEA @ NeurIPS’25) · DRACO (preprint) · Hybrid-Gym (ICML’26)

  • Efficiency and failure mitigation. I design lightweight critics and strong-weak collaboration schemes that steer large code agents at a fraction of the cost, and I measure wasted effort such as code slop in agent-generated patches.
    Steer, Don’t Solve (EMNLP’26) · Strong-Weak Collaboration (EMNLP’25) · Code Slop (WAB @ COLM’26)

  • Reliability and security of code generation. I work on utility-preserving defenses for code LLMs, red-teaming of code agents, and detection and repair of security vulnerabilities, including as part of the CMU team in the Amazon Nova AI Challenge (Trusted AI ‘25 finalist, Trusted Software Agents ‘26 red team lead).
    Secure and Useful Models are Reasonable (Amazon Nova ‘25) · ABSENTIA (under review) · StaticFixer (preprint)

I also serve as a Teaching Assistant for the MCDS Capstone courses at CMU and reviewed for ARR, EDM, and SEA @ NeurIPS.
